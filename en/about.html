About & Copyright

What is STS2Guides?

STS2Guides is an unofficial, fan-made strategy and reference site for Slay the Spire 2. We provide card databases, tier lists, boss strategies, character guides, and relic references — all written from our own gameplay experience and community knowledge.

Our goal is to help players learn the game faster and enjoy it more deeply.
