🧠 Strategy & Tips

X-cost AoE damage. Best with energy generation (Offering, Bloodletting). Scales with Strength for massive AoE clears.

🎯 When to Pick

Situationally strong. Pick this when it fits your deck's strategy or you need its specific effect.

As an Attack card, it directly contributes to your damage output. Evaluate if your deck needs more frontloaded damage or scaling damage.

🤝 Synergies

Ironclad excels at high-damage attacks and self-damage synergies. Cards that provide Strength scaling, exhaust effects, or healing pair well with Ironclad's kit.

Attack cards benefit from Strength, Vulnerable, and damage-multiplying relics. Pair with energy-cheap Attacks for maximum efficiency.
