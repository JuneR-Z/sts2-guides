Death March

B-Tier 💀 Necrobinder

Attack

Uncommon

📝 Effect

Osty deals 10 damage. Deal 4 additional damage per Exhausted card this combat.

⬆️ Upgrade

Osty deals 15 damage. Deal 4(6) additional damage per Exhausted card this combat.

📊 Tier Rating Rationale

v0.106.0: additional damage increased from 3(4) to 4(6). Scales harder in Exhaust-heavy runs.

🧠 Strategy & Tips

Osty attack that scales with Exhausted cards. With v0.106.0 buff to 4(6) per Exhausted card, it scales significantly faster - 10 Exhausted cards = 40(60) bonus damage. Core Necrobinder damage card. Synergizes with Forbidden Grimoire and Souls mechanic for massive scaling.

🎯 When to Pick

Situationally strong. Pick this when it fits your deck's strategy or you need its specific effect.

As an Attack card, it directly contributes to your damage output. Evaluate if your deck needs more frontloaded damage or scaling damage.

One-cost cards are the backbone of most decks. They are easy to fit into any turn without straining your Energy budget.

🤝 Synergies

Necrobinder manipulates Doom stacks and executes. Cards that apply Doom, or trigger Death-knell effects, synergize with Necrobinder's unique mechanics.

Attack cards benefit from Strength, Vulnerable, and damage-multiplying relics. Pair with energy-cheap Attacks for maximum efficiency.
