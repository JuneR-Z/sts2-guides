Overview

The Defect channels elemental Orbs — Lightning (damage), Frost (block), Dark (accumulating damage), and Plasma (energy) — that provide passive effects each turn. The key to Defect is Focus, which amplifies all orb effects. Proper orb slot management and Focus stacking elevates Defect from mediocre to game-breaking.

Core Mechanics

🔮 Orb Management

Channel Lightning for passive damage, Frost for passive block, Dark for accumulating burst damage, and Plasma for energy. Evoke orbs with Dualcast or Multicast for powerful burst effects. Aim for 5+ orb slots.

🎯 Focus Scaling

Focus is Defect's most important stat — it increases all orb effectiveness. Stack Focus with Defragment, Biased Cognition + artifact source (Core Surge/Orange Pellets), or Consume + Capacitor.

⚡ Power Synergy

Defect has the strongest Power cards. Creative AI generates Powers each turn. Echo Form doubles your first card each turn. Storm channels Lightning whenever you play a Power — build your entire strategy around it.

Pro Tips

  • Prioritize orb slot expansion. 3 slots is not enough — aim for 5+ with Capacitor or Runic Capacitor.
  • Biased Cognition + artifact source is Defect's strongest combo. Buy Orange Pellets if you see it in a shop.
  • Frost orbs are mandatory for high Ascension. One Glacier is worth more than three Defends.
  • In Act 1, take attack cards. Starting with only Zap + Dualcast means you need more damage to beat elites.
  • Seek is perhaps the best card in the game. Upgrade it to fetch 2 cards from your draw pile at 0 cost.
  • Echo Form + Self Repair doubles your healing. Buffer + Echo Form prevents the next 2 hits.
