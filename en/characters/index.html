Choose Your Character

3 returning classics + 2 brand-new heroes. Every character supports multiple viable archetypes.
Updated Jun 3 — Official stats: 240M+ global runs · 25% overall WR · A0 16% (STS1 9%) · A10 ~17%. ~80K concurrent (Steam). Community data (23,155+ runs, v0.106.1 meta + Devour the Tower tournament). Silent Sly Discard dominates at 35.6% WR. Next patch ~Jun 11-12.

⚔️

Ironclad

Strength · Block · Exhaust · Self-Damage
The most straightforward character. Stack massive Strength, build impenetrable block, or exhaust your entire deck for infinite combos. Built-in healing makes him the best choice for beginners. Exhaust engine remains the most consistent win condition in EA.

Community WR: 31.7% · #3 · → Stable · Exhaust engine still most consistent A20 build
Exhaust S Strength A Block A
🗡️

Silent

Sly · Discard · Poison · Shiv
Master of precision and action economy. The new Sly mechanic (Tingsha + Tough Bandages) generates free cards on discard for infinite block/damage engines. Poison with Catalyst still scales exponentially. Shiv synergies remain strong but Sly Discard is now the meta-defining build.

Community WR: 35.6% · 👑 #1 · ↑19.9% · Sly Discard breaks action economy
Sly Discard S+ Poison S Shiv A
⚡

Defect

Orbs · Claw · Sync · Powers
Channel elemental Orbs for passive damage and block. Claw Machine (0-cost cycling) is the current strongest build. Synchronize burst offers explosive turns. Focus stacking was reworked — now relies on orb cycling speed rather than permanent scaling. High ceiling, requires precise planning.

Community WR: 29.8% · #5 · ↑15.6% · Claw Machine build rising in popularity
Claw Machine S Sync Burst A Frost Focus A

Necrobinder

Doom · Summon · Soul Engine
New to STS2! Doom execution skips enemy HP scaling with one-turn kill combos. Summon armies of Skeletons, Ghosts, and Abominations for board control. Bank Souls for devastating finishers. Multiple S-tier archetypes: Doom Execute, Soul/Haunt, and Curse Master. Most versatile new character.

Community WR: 32.1% · #2 · ↑9.8% · Devour the Tower champion, Doom meta-defining
Doom Execute S+ Soul/Haunt S Summon A
NEW
👑

Regent

Stars · Forge · Arsenal · Positioning
A tactical commander who manages two distinct engines: Stars (cross-round retained resources for explosive Aligned turns) and Forge (equipping Sovereign's Blade for late-game domination). Do not mix engines! High skill cap — Stars Alignment build now competitive after v0.104 Arsenal rework.

Community WR: 30.4% · #4 · ↑22.0% · Massive recovery after Arsenal buffs — no longer bottom tier
Stars Engine S Forge A 7-Stars AOE A
