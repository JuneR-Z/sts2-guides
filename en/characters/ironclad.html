Overview

The Ironclad is the most straightforward character in Slay the Spire 2, built around raw strength, defensive stacking, and high-risk self-damage synergies. His starting relic, Burning Blood (heal 6 HP after each combat), provides built-in sustain that forgives misplays — making him the ideal choice for beginners. At higher Ascensions, master his exhaust engine and strength scaling to unleash devastating one-turn kills.

Core Mechanics

⚡ Strength Scaling

Stack Strength via Demon Form, Spot Weakness, or Inflame to multiply damage on multi-hit attacks like Heavy Blade and Twin Strike. At high Ascension, Strength defines Ironclad's win condition.

🔥 Exhaust Engine

Use Corruption, Feel No Pain, and Dark Embrace to thin your deck mid-combat while generating block and draw. Exhausting cards is not a downside — it's a win condition.

🩸 Self-Damage Synergy

Cards like Hemokinesis, Bloodletting, and Offering trade HP for powerful effects. Pair with Rupture to gain Strength whenever you lose HP from cards.

🛡️ Block Engine

Barricade prevents block from decaying between turns. Entrench doubles your block. Body Slam converts all that block into direct damage — one of the safest win conditions in the game.

Strength Build

Stack massive Strength and multiply it with Limit Break. Heavy Blade scales 3× with Strength for one-shot potential. The most consistent high-Ascension build.

B-TIER

Self-Damage Engine

Trade HP for devastating effects. Hemokinesis deals high damage at low cost. Bloodletting and Offering generate energy at HP cost. Rupture grants Strength whenever you lose HP from cards. Sustain the HP drain with Reaper — the more Strength you have, the more you heal.

Rupture Hemokinesis Offering Bloodletting Reaper Combust
Runic Cube Magic Flower Meal Ticket
B-TIER

Perfected Strike

Collect every "Strike" card for massive Perfected Strike damage — each Strike in your deck adds 2 base damage. Twin Strike and Pommel Strike provide extra value. Simple to pilot but highly effective through mid-Ascension. Puny decks skip this; thick decks love it.

Perfected Strike Twin Strike Pommel Strike Wild Strike Thunderclap Headbutt
Snecko Eye Necronomicon

Pro Tips

  • Prioritize removing Strikes over Defends — Ironclad's attack cards are far stronger than basics.
  • Self-damage cards are excellent but require sustain. Burning Blood helps offset the cost.
  • Limit Break+ can be doubled again by cycling it back with Headbutt.
  • In Act 1, prioritize strong attack cards (Carnage, Hemokinesis) to handle elites like Gremlin Nob.
  • Whirlwind+ is an excellent AoE solution for multi-enemy fights and Slime Boss.
  • Offering is one of the best cards in the game — take it every time you see it.
  • Reaper + Strength provides massive healing. Save it for when you need HP recovery.
