Overview

New to Slay the Spire 2, the Necrobinder summons minions to fight alongside her. Minions can tank hits, deal damage, or be sacrificed for powerful effects. The Soul mechanic, fueled by minion deaths, is her primary resource for high-impact cards. She's a high-skill-cap character who rewards careful board management and sacrifice timing.

Core Mechanics

💀 Summon Army

Summon Skeletons (taunt/tank), Ghosts (passive damage), and Abominations (heavy hitters) to your side. Each minion type has different stats and effects. Max 4 minion slots — manage your board carefully.

🩸 Sacrifice Engine

Destroy your own minions for powerful effects: draw cards, gain energy, deal burst damage, or heal. Every sacrifice fuels your Soul counter, which unlocks your strongest finishers.

🔮 Soul Economy

Souls are your secondary resource. Spend accumulated Souls on high-impact cards like Soul Nova (AoE damage per Soul) and Death Pact (heal + soul gain). Banking Souls for the right moment defines the Necrobinder playstyle.

Pro Tips

  • Minions ARE your block. Skeletons taunt enemies — let them soak hits instead of spending energy on Defends.
  • Don't sacrifice all your minions at once. Always keep at least one tank on the board to absorb damage.
  • Soul generation is your win condition. Prioritize cards that generate or efficiently spend Souls.
  • Necrobinder struggles in Act 1. Play conservatively until your minion engine comes online.
  • Undead Commander is your most important Power — it makes every minion significantly stronger.
  • In boss fights, bank Souls for 5+ turns, then unleash Soul Nova for massive burst damage.
