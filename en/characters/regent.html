Overview

The Regent is a tactical commander who issues Commands each turn to control the battlefield. Master the Sly mechanic to outmaneuver enemies — Sly increases crit chance and unlocks powerful bonus effects. Stars accumulate from consistent Command execution and persist between combats, powering devastating finishers like Starfall. Complex but incredibly rewarding for players who enjoy tactical decision-making.

Core Mechanics

📜 Command System

Each turn, your Royal Decree relic offers a Command choice (Strike, Defend, Balance, etc.). Following it grants Sly stacks and bonus effects. Tactical Genius gives more Command options — increasing your chance of a good match.

🦊 Sly Mechanic

Sly represents tactical advantage. Each stack increases crit chance and unlocks bonus effects on Command cards. At 5 Sly stacks, all attacks ignore enemy block entirely. Manage Sly like a second resource bar.

⭐ Star Scaling

Stars accumulate when you perfectly execute Commands. At 10 Stars, unleash Starfall — a massive AoE finisher. Stars persist between combats, rewarding consistent play. Enter boss fights with high Stars for a massive advantage.

Pro Tips

  • Always check your Command at the start of each turn before playing any cards.
  • Breaking Command is sometimes correct — if following it would kill you, ignore it and survive.
  • Sly stacks are your most valuable resource. Cards that generate Sly without requiring Commands are premium picks.
  • Stars persist between fights. Plan your path to enter boss fights with high Star count for a huge Starfall.
  • Regent is weakest in short hallway fights. She truly shines in elite and boss encounters where she can build momentum.
  • Tactical Genius is the single best upgrade — more Command choices = more consistency and more Stars.
