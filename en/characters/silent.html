Overview

The Silent excels at precision damage through Poison stacking and rapid Shiv attacks. Her starting relic, Ring of the Snake (draw 2 extra cards on turn 1), gives her excellent opening consistency. Her larger starting deck and defensive lean require careful card selection in Act 1, but she scales into one of the most powerful characters in the late game.

Core Mechanics

☠️ Poison Stacking

Apply Poison that deals damage each turn. Catalyst doubles or triples existing Poison — the core of exponential damage scaling. Noxious Fumes provides passive, artifact-proof Poison application.

🔪 Shiv Spam

Generate 0-cost Shivs with Blade Dance and Cloak and Dagger. Pair with Accuracy for damage boosts, or leverage Wrist Blade (+4 to 0-cost attacks). Shuriken/Kunai proc on 3 attacks per turn.

🃏 Discard Synergy

Cycle your deck with Acrobatics and Calculated Gamble. Tactician generates energy on discard. Reflex draws cards. Tingsha and Tough Bandages convert discards into damage and block.

Pro Tips

  • Silent struggles in Act 1 — prioritize strong attack cards like Dagger Spray before the first elite fight.
  • Well Laid Plans is one of her best cards. Retaining a Catalyst or key card wins fights.
  • Upgrade Neutralize early. 2 turns of Weak dramatically reduces incoming damage.
  • Don't over-commit to one archetype too early. Silent benefits from blending Poison, Shiv, and discard elements.
  • Wraith Form is the best defensive card in the game — 3 turns of Intangible makes you nearly invincible.
  • Nightmare + Catalyst can generate 3 Catalysts next turn for exponential poison multiplication.
