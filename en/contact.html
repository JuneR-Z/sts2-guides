DMCA Notice Requirements

For a valid DMCA takedown request, please include the following in your notice to dmca@sts2guides.com:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work — specify the original work you claim has been infringed
  • Identification of the infringing material — provide the exact URL(s) on sts2guides.com
  • Your contact information — full name, email address, and phone number
  • Good faith statement — a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner
  • Accuracy statement — a statement under penalty of perjury that the information in your notice is accurate
  • Signature — a physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent
