🏛️ Ancient Boons & Alternate Chapters: Complete System Guide

📅 Updated Jun 3, 2026 · ⏱️ 11 min read · 🏷️ Mechanics v0.106.1

Ancient Boons are STS2's new chapter-variant system — replacing the STS1 Act structure with dynamic, choice-based alternate chapters. At the start of Acts 2 and 3, you choose between the standard chapter and an Ancient chapter, each offering unique enemies, events, and a powerful Ancient Boon reward on completion.

How Alternate Chapters Work

At the start of Act 2 and Act 3, you face a fork:

  • Standard Chapter: Normal enemies, events, and boss
  • Ancient Chapter: Harder enemies (Ancient variants), unique ? events, different boss, but rewards an Ancient Boon on completion

Ancient chapters are risk/reward: harder fights + a powerful permanent buff. The question isn't just "which boon is best" — it's "can I survive the Ancient chapter with my current deck?"

When to Take the Ancient Route (vs Safe Route)

Take Ancient Chapter IF:

  • ✅ Your deck is ahead of the curve (beat last elite with 60%+ HP)
  • ✅ You have sustain/recovery (healing relic, sustain cards)
  • ✅ Your deck counters Ancient enemy types (AOE for swarm fights)
  • ✅ The boon directly synergizes with your build (Focus boon for Defect)

Take Standard Chapter IF:

  • ❌ You're low HP (below 50%) or just scraped by the last boss
  • ❌ Your deck needs time to scale and Ancient enemies will punish you
  • ❌ The offered boon doesn't help your build (Fury boon for Frost Defect)
  • ❌ You're playing Defect with no Frost setup — Ancient elites will destroy you
