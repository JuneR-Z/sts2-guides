🤝 Co-op Team Composition Tier List: Best 2/3/4-Player Combos

📅 Updated Jun 3, 2026 · ⏱️ 13 min read · 🏷️ Tier List Co-op

Post-Devour the Tower, the co-op meta has crystallized. 34 streamers ran 17 team comps through 48+ hours of high-stakes co-op. These are the team compositions that work — ranked by community consensus and tournament results.

The Four Co-op Roles

RolePrimary JobBest Characters
🛡️ TankAbsorb shared damage, generate party blockIronclad, Defect (Frost)
⚔️ DPSBurst damage, boss killingSilent, Necrobinder
🔮 SupportBuff party, debuff enemies, enable combosRegent, Silent
🔄 FlexAdapt to what the team needsDefect, Ironclad

2-Player (Duo) Tier List

A-Tier Duos

DuoSynergy
A Silent (Poison) + Necrobinder (Doom)Double HP-scaling damage. Both bypass block. Bosses die in 3 turns.
A Ironclad (Strength) + Defect (Frost)Defect blocks, Ironclad smashes. Classic carry-support dynamic. Safe but slow.
A Regent (Sly Control) + Silent (Sly Discard)Double Sly synergy! Shared Sly buffs, infinite card draw, enemy debuff stacking. Requires coordination.

B-Tier Duos (Viable but Suboptimal)

DuoWeakness
Ironclad + RegentNo shared damage type synergy. Ironclad wants to tank, Regent wants to scale — they don't help each other.
Defect + NecrobinderBoth want to set up. Too slow — elites punish you before either character comes online.
Silent + SilentMirror comp. One Silent will get contested on Sly/Discard cards. Food chain problem in shared card pools.

Co-op Drafting Rules

  1. Avoid overlapping same-color cards: Two Defects contest Focus cards. Two Silents contest Sly cards. The shared card pool is zero-sum.
  2. One tank minimum: Shared damage in co-op means someone MUST absorb hits. Ironclad or Frost Defect are mandatory in 3+ player teams.
  3. Spell-based synergy: Cards that affect "all enemies" or "all allies" have multiplied value in co-op. Electrodynamics, Corruption, After Image.
  4. Communication beats tier lists: A well-coordinated B-tier duo beats a silent S-tier duo every time. Discuss turn order, buff timing, and target priority.
🎯 Key Takeaway: Silent + Necrobinder is the undisputed king of 2-player co-op (41.2% tournament WR). For 4-player, Silent/Necrobinder/Defect/Ironclad covers every role without card pool conflicts. The #1 co-op mistake is double-dipping on the same character's card pool.
