Defect vs Necrobinder: Orb Management vs Summon Armies

~9 min read · Comparing STS2's two unique-resource characters — orbs vs summons, consistency vs ceiling

Quick Verdict

🏆 Winner by category: Defect wins in consistency, ease of play, and defensive reliability. Necrobinder wins in scaling potential, unique playstyle, and boss-killing power. Defect is the safe pick; Necrobinder is the high-ceiling pick.

Core Mechanic Comparison

Defect: Orb Management

Defect's power comes from channeling and evoking orbs. Each orb type provides passive effects (Lightning = damage, Frost = block, Dark = accumulating damage, Plasma = energy, Glass = AoE damage on evoke). The key skills are orb slot management (Capacitor) and Focus scaling (Defragment, Biased Cognition).

Strength: Orbs provide consistent value every turn without spending energy. Once your orb setup is complete, you "solve" the fight — the orbs handle offense and defense while you play Powers.

Weakness: Slow setup. Without early Focus or orb generation, Defect takes chip damage while building infrastructure. Vulnerable to fast, aggressive enemies in Act 1-2.

Necrobinder: Summon & Sacrifice

Necrobinder commands Osty (permanent summon) and generates temporary summons. Her mechanics revolve around death triggers: when Osty or summons die, they fuel Soul generation, Doom application, and sacrifice payoffs. Doom is a delayed damage mechanic — stack it on enemies and watch them die over time.

Strength: Total damage output is the highest in the game with proper setup. Doom + Reaper Form creates an unstoppable clock. Sacrifice mechanics (Sacrifice card, Osty death triggers) generate massive value spikes.

Weakness: Extremely fragile if Osty dies at the wrong time. Draw-order dependent — if you draw setup cards (Doom, Summon) and payoff cards (Death triggers) in the wrong sequence, you lose. Complexity makes pilot errors common.
