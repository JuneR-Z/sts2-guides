Gold & Shop Strategy: When to Buy, When to Save, What to Prioritize

Master Slay the Spire 2's economy. Learn optimal shopping decisions, relic valuation, card removal math, and how to squeeze maximum value from every run's gold budget.

Beginner-Friendly Economy Guide 🕐 12 min read 📅 Updated: May 2026

Gold is the most flexible resource in Slay the Spire 2. Unlike card rewards or relics — which are random — gold gives you agency. You choose what to buy. Bad shop decisions cost runs. Great shop decisions win runs at A20 where margins are razor-thin.

Gold Income: Where Does It Come From?

A typical A20 run earns 400–650 total gold (not counting Old Coin/Golden Idol). That's ~5–7 shop visits across 3 acts.

F-Tier: Never Buy

  • Dream Catcher — Resting is already a desperation play. Don't pay gold for it.
  • Darkstone Periapt — You don't WANT curses. This rewards losing.
  • The Specimen — Too situational; Corpse Explosion does the same job for free.
  • Bottled Flame — Bottling an Attack sounds good until you brick your turn 1 draw for the rest of the run.

When to Buy Cards From the Shop

The general rule: don't buy cards unless they immediately upgrade your win condition. You get free card rewards from fights; gold is for relics and removals.

🛒 The "Apotheosis Rule": If Apotheosis (upgrades all cards for the rest of combat) is in the shop, always buy it unless you already have one. It's the single best colorless card in the game and single-handedly wins runs. Cost: ~200g. Worth: infinite.

Potions: Hidden Shop Value

Potions are cheap (48–60g) and save runs. The shop is your guaranteed potion source — you'll find maybe 2–3 potions from fights per act.

🧴 Potion Strategy: Don't hoard potions "for the final boss." Use them on Elites. An Elite kill = relic + gold + card + potion — a potion that secures an Elite kill is a net positive investment of 48g. The most common A20 mistake is dying with 3 potions.

Act-by-Act Shopping Strategy

Act 1 Shop (Early — Floor 1–6)

  • Priority: Buy a powerful Attack card if you're going into an Elite path. Potions.
  • Skip: Don't remove cards yet — 75g for one removal is half your gold. Wait.
  • Don't hoard: If you find an S-tier relic and can afford it, buy it. Early power compounds.
  • Jackpot finds: Apotheosis, Membership Card, Orange Pellets, any energy relic

Act 1 Boss → Act 2 Shop (Critical Moment)

  • You just got ~100g from the Boss. You probably have 250–350g total.
  • This is your most important shop. Buy an S/A-tier relic or premium rare card.
  • NOW remove a Strike/Defend. Your deck is taking shape.
  • If you're weak on AoE, buy an AoE card NOW. Act 2 will punish you hard without it.

Act 2–3: Stay Flexible

  • By now your deck should have a clear identity. Buy relics that amplify it.
  • Card removal accelerates: At 150–200g per removal, the Smiling Mask relic becomes very appealing.
  • Stock up on potions before Act 3 Boss gauntlet.

Common Mistakes

  1. Buying "nice to have" relics instead of saving for an S-tier: 150g on a common relic you don't need = you can't afford the 280g Dead Branch in the next shop.
  2. Skipping card removal: At A20, those 3 Strikes in your deck are actively losing you the run. Remove them.
  3. Overvaluing colorless cards: Most colorless cards are mediocre and overpriced. Only Apotheosis, Master of Strategy, Panache, and The Bomb justify their cost.
  4. Shopping too early on Act 1: You have ~100–150g. Buying a common card is fine. Buying a relic leaves you broke and unable to afford the Act 2 shop with 300g.
  5. Ignoring potions: 50g for a Ghost in a Jar that saves your run? That's a relic you paid 50g for.
  6. Taking Ectoplasm: Just don't. Energy is not worth losing the entire economy. Coffee Dripper (no rests) or Fusion Hammer (no smithing) are strictly better.

Key Takeaways

  1. Gold priority: S-tier relics > card removal > key archetype cards > potions > A-tier relics > everything else
  2. The Act 2 Shop is the most important. You have ~300g, your deck needs direction. Make it count.
  3. Card removal wins runs. Removing all Strikes is the single biggest power increase in the game.
  4. Buy potions, use them on Elites. A 50g potion that secures an Elite kill is the best ROI in STS2.
  5. Never take Ectoplasm. Zero gold means you can't remove cards, can't buy relics, can't buy potions. It's a trap.
  6. Membership Card is the best relic in the game. If you find it in a shop, buy it immediately. It pays for itself within 1–2 purchases.
