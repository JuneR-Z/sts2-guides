Ironclad vs Silent: Which STS2 Character Should You Main?

~10 min read · Full side-by-side comparison of STS2's two original characters

Quick Verdict

🏆 Winner by category: Ironclad wins in ease of use, consistency, and new-player friendliness. Silent wins in ceiling potential, complexity, and satisfying combos. If you're new to STS2: pick Ironclad. If you're a veteran looking for depth: pick Silent.

Head-to-Head Comparison

🔴 IRONCLAD

  • HP: 80 (highest base HP)
  • Starting Relic: Burning Blood (heal 6 HP after combat)
  • Core Mechanic: Strength scaling + HP trades
  • Playstyle: Aggressive, straightforward, forgiving
  • Difficulty: ⭐⭐ (Easy)

🟢 SILENT

  • HP: 70 (lowest base HP)
  • Starting Relic: Ring of the Snake (draw 2 extra cards on turn 1)
  • Core Mechanic: Poison + Shiv + Discard cycle
  • Playstyle: Methodical, precise, combo-oriented
  • Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Hard)

Archetype Breakdown

Ironclad's Top 3 Builds

  1. Strength Scaling — Demon Form + Limit Break + Heavy Blade. Straightforward: stack Strength, hit hard. Best for learning Ironclad.
  2. Exhaust Engine — Corruption + Feel No Pain + Dark Embrace. Converts Skills into free block + card draw. Highest win rate at A20. Full guide →
  3. Block Ramp — Barricade + Body Slam + Entrench. Turns defense into offense. Strong vs bosses, slow vs multi-enemy fights.

Silent's Top 3 Builds

  1. Poison Catalyst — Deadly Poison + Catalyst + Burst. Apply poison, then multiply it exponentially. 999+ Poison turns are common. Full guide →
  2. Shiv Storm — Blade Dance + Accuracy + Wrist Blade + Afterimage. Spam free attacks while generating block. Full guide →
  3. Discard Cycle — Calculated Gamble + Reflex + Tactician + Acrobatics. Cycle your entire deck every turn. High skill ceiling, infinite potential.

🎮 Personal Recommendation: Start with Ironclad to learn the game's fundamentals (pathing, card evaluation, energy management). Once you've won a few runs and understand enemy patterns, switch to Silent — she'll teach you deck manipulation and combo execution at a level Ironclad never requires. Both characters are worth mastering, but the learning curve order should be Ironclad → Silent.
