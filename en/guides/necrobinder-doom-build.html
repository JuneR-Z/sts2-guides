Necrobinder Doom Execute Build: Skip HP Scaling with One-Turn Kill Combos

🏆 Build Guide · Updated May 28, 2026 · 10 min read · S-Tier — #1 A20+ Meta

The Doom Execute build is currently the #1 Ascension 20+ clearing strategy in Slay the Spire 2. By leveraging the Necrobinder's unique Doom mechanic, you can execute enemies at a percentage HP threshold — completely bypassing enemy HP scaling at high Ascension levels. When enemies have 400+ HP on A20, a 40% execute threshold is the most efficient damage in the game. This guide covers the full combo, best cards, relic synergies, and act-by-act strategy.

What Is the Doom Mechanic?

Doom is a debuff unique to the Necrobinder. When an enemy has Doom stacks, they become executable — if their HP falls below a certain percentage, they die instantly regardless of remaining HP. Each Doom stack increases the execute threshold:

  • 1-2 Doom stacks: Execute at 15-20% HP — decent but inconsistent
  • 3-4 Doom stacks: Execute at 25-35% HP — your build comes online
  • 5+ Doom stacks: Execute at 40-50% HP — bosses die at half health

At A20, enemies have +50% HP. A boss with 500 HP effectively has 750 HP. The Doom build ignores that extra 175 HP entirely — you only need to deal 250 damage instead of 500. That's a 50% effective damage multiplier that scales with enemy HP, making this the only build that gets stronger at higher Ascensions.

💀 The One-Turn Execute Combo

Setup Turn: Apply Death Sentence (3 Doom stacks) + Mark of the End (2 more stacks). Enemy now has 5 Doom = 40% execute threshold.
Execute Turn: Play Grim Toll (deal damage + trigger execute if below threshold). If the enemy is below 40% HP, they die instantly.
Boss Variant: Add Inevitable Fate (double current Doom stacks). 5 Doom → 10 Doom = 55% execute threshold. Most bosses die before their second attack cycle.

Core Cards

Must-Have (S-Tier)

  • Death Sentence — Apply 3 Doom stacks. Upgraded: 4 stacks. This is your primary Doom source, the Corruption of the Doom build. Take it every time you see it.
  • Grim Toll — Deal 12 damage. If the target has Doom and is below the threshold, execute them. Upgraded: 18 damage + applies 1 Doom before the strike. This card ends fights.
  • Mark of the End — Apply 2 Doom stacks to ALL enemies. Upgraded: 3 to all. Essential for multi-enemy fights. The AoE Doom application is unmatched.
  • Inevitable Fate — Double the Doom stacks on target enemy. Exhaust. This card turns a 3-stack Doom into a 6-stack. With Death Sentence + this, you hit the cap in one turn.

Strong Support (A-Tier)

  • Soul Harvest — Deal 8 damage per Soul. Generates Souls as you cycle Doom cards. Provides consistent chip damage to bring enemies into execute range.
  • Bone Legion — Summon 2 Skeletons. Your defense package — Skeletons taunt and tank while you stack Doom. You don't want to waste energy on block cards.
  • Gravebind — Target enemy cannot heal and takes +25% damage from all sources. Makes reaching the execute threshold 25% faster. Also shuts down healing enemies like the Champ.
  • Dark Offering — Sacrifice a minion, draw 2 cards, gain 2 energy. Cycle acceleration — crucial for finding your Doom pieces in a thick deck.

The Doom Math: Why This Build Scales Better Than Anything Else

Let's compare a traditional damage build versus Doom at A20:

  • Strength Ironclad (Limit Break + Heavy Blade): Needs ~500 raw damage to kill a 500 HP boss. Requires 10+ turns of scaling.
  • Poison Silent (Catalyst x3): Needs ~500 poison damage. Requires 8-10 turns of stacking and triple Catalyst.
  • Doom Necrobinder (Death Sentence x2 + Grim Toll): Needs ~250 damage to hit 50% threshold, then execute. Requires 4-5 turns.

The difference becomes more extreme at A20 where enemy HP is inflated. While other builds need to scale their damage output by 50%, the Doom build's execute threshold scales with the inflated HP — you always need the same number of turns regardless of Ascension level.

💡 Pro Tip — Soul Economy: Souls are generated whenever you apply a debuff, including Doom. This means your Doom application cycle naturally generates the resource needed for Soul Harvest. Aim to have 3-4 Souls before unleashing Soul Harvest to bring the boss within execute range.

Best Relics

  • Hourglass of Ruin (S) — At the start of your turn, apply 1 Doom to a random enemy. Free Doom generation every turn without spending energy. The single best relic for this build.
  • Doom Bell (S) — When you execute an enemy, apply 1 Doom to all remaining enemies. Creates chain executes in multi-enemy fights.
  • Soul Vessel (A) — Start combat with 3 Souls. Jump-starts your Soul economy for early Soul Harvest.
  • Philosopher's Stone (Boss Relic) (A) — +1 energy per turn. The Doom build is energy-hungry — you want to play Death Sentence, Mark, and Grim Toll in the same turn.
  • Preserved Insect (B) — Enemies in Elite combats have -25% HP. Makes the execute threshold even easier to reach against the toughest fights.
⚠️ Common Mistake: Don't tunnel-vision on Doom cards and forget defense. You still need to survive the turns it takes to stack Doom. At minimum, take 2-3 skeleton-summoning cards (Bone Legion, Raise Dead) to tank hits while you set up. A dead Necrobinder applies zero Doom.

Run Strategy — Stage by Stage

  1. Floors 1-8 (Early Act 1):

    Necrobinder's Act 1 is about survival. Take Bone Throw and Raise Dead for early damage and defense. You won't have Doom cards yet — that's normal. Prioritize upgrading defense cards so you survive to see better card rewards. If you see Death Sentence in a card reward, grab it immediately even if the rest of your deck isn't ready.

  2. Floors 9-16 (Late Act 1):

    This is where the transition begins. By now you should have 1-2 Doom cards. Mark of the End is excellent AoE Doom for the Act 1 boss fights (Slime Boss and Guardian both benefit from AoE application). Grab your first Grim Toll — this card makes the build functional.

  3. Floors 17-25 (Early Act 2):

    The build accelerates. Inevitable Fate is your #1 priority. One Death Sentence + Inevitable Fate = 6-8 Doom stacks = 45-50% execute threshold. Act 2 elites (Gremlin Leader, Slavers) are multi-enemy fights where Mark of the End shines. By the end of early Act 2, your deck should have 4-5 Doom cards.

  4. Floors 26-33 (Late Act 2):

    Refine your deck. Remove Strikes — you're not dealing conventional damage anymore. Dark Offering for card draw helps you cycle to your Doom pieces faster. Look for Gravebind to amplify damage against bosses. The Collector boss fight is your first real Doom test — apply Mark of the End to hit both Collector and his minions.

  5. Floors 34-50 (Act 3):

    Peak Doom power. You should have 6-8 Doom cards and enough defense to survive 4-5 turns of setup. Against Time Eater, Doom's execute bypasses the 12-card limit — you can kill him with 5 well-planned cards. Against the Awakened One, apply Doom in phase 1, execute, then repeat in phase 2. The Doom build is arguably the strongest A3 clearer in the current meta.

Matchup Notes

  • vs Gremlin Nob: Neutral matchup. Doom application cards (Death Sentence, Mark) are Skills that trigger his Strength. Minimize Skill usage and rely on your Skeleton minions to tank. Once he's below 30%, execute with Grim Toll.
  • vs Slime Boss: Excellent matchup. Apply Mark of the End to all slimes after the split. The 3 Doom on all targets means you can execute each small slime at 25% HP — almost trivializing the phase.
  • vs Collector: Good matchup. Mark of the End hits all 3 enemies. Execute the Collector's minion first to trigger Doom Bell chain executes, then focus on the Collector.
  • vs Time Eater: Excellent matchup. Doom executes don't require playing many cards in a turn — apply Doom (1 card), chip damage (1 card), execute (1 card). The 12-card limit barely matters.
  • vs Corrupted Heart: Strong. Apply Doom in the first cycle, tank the big attack with Skeletons, then execute before the second cycle. Gravebind prevents the Heart's healing.
