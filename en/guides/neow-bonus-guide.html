🐳 Neow's Blessing Tier List: Best Starter Bonuses for Every Character

📅 Updated Jun 3, 2026 · ⏱️ 10 min read · 🏷️ Tier List v0.106.1

Neow's Blessing is your first decision every run — and it can make or break a character. Choosing the wrong bonus for your character can leave you dead Act 1. This guide ranks every Neow bonus for each of the 5 characters.

How Neow's Blessing Works in STS2

At the start of each run, Neow offers 4 options (2 if you died in Act 1 on the previous run). Bonuses are grouped into:

  • Positive-only (no downside): Small but safe bonuses
  • Risk/reward (with downside): Big bonus + penalty (lose HP, curse, gold)
  • Boss relic swap: Trade your starter relic for a random boss relic — highest variance pick

S-Tier — Always Take These

BonusBest CharacterWhy It's S-Tier
Transform 2 cardsAll charactersRemoves 2 Starter cards + gives 2 random cards. Always increases deck quality. No downside.
Remove 2 cardsSilent, DefectSlims deck to 8-9 cards. Defect can remove 2 Strikes for immediate orb consistency.
Rare card choiceIronclad (Corruption), Silent (Wraith Form), Defect (Echo Form)High-rolling a build-defining rare wins runs. Even a mid-roll rare is better than a Strike.
250 Gold (lose 50% HP)Ironclad, NecrobinderWith Burning Blood heal, 250g buys a rare relic or 2-3 early cards. Worth the HP.
Boss relic swapDefect (Cracked Core → Energy relic), Silent (Ring → 4th energy)Highest win-rate option on high-ascension. Defect gets +1 energy, Silent gets a better relic.

Per-Character Neow Priority

Ironclad

1st choice: Boss relic swap (high-roll energy relic). 2nd: Rare card (Corruption/Offering). 3rd: 250 Gold penalty. Burning Blood sustain makes HP penalties safer.

Silent

1st choice: Transform 2. 2nd: Remove 2 (dump 2 Defends). 3rd: Boss relic swap (Ring of the Snake isn't that good). Silent values deck quality over brute force.

Defect

1st choice: Boss relic swap (Cracked Core → energy). 2nd: Upgrade a card (Zap+). 3rd: Rare card. Defect's starter deck is weak — any improvement helps.

Necrobinder

1st choice: Rare card. 2nd: Transform 2. 3rd: Max HP +. Necrobinder's power spikes come from minion cards — finding one early snowballs.

Regent

1st choice: Transform 2 (trim the complex starter deck). 2nd: Rare card. 3rd: Max HP +. Regent's starter deck is the largest — trimming is essential.

🎯 Key Takeaway: The best Neow bonus depends on your character, not a universal tier list. Boss relic swap is S-tier on Defect, D-tier on Necrobinder. Transform 2 is the safest universally strong pick. Never take triple-penalty bonuses — the downside snowballs into a dead run by mid-Act 1.
