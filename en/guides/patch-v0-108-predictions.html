v0.108.0 Predictions: The Nerfs, Buffs & Meta Shakeup We Expect (June 18)

🔮 Predictions · Updated Jun 13, 2026 · 7 min read · 5 Days to Patch Day

With 5 days until the expected June 18 v0.108.0 beta, the STS2 community is in full speculation mode. v0.107.0 was a deliberately small "stability-first" patch — Mega Crit's own words. That means v0.108.0 is primed to be the first substantial balance patch in a month (since v0.106.0 on May 22). Here's our comprehensive prediction breakdown, sourced from Reddit, Discord, and community win-rate data.

📊 Methodology

Predictions are based on: community consensus (Reddit r/slaythespire2 + Discord polls), win-rate outliers from Week 24-25 meta data, developer pattern analysis (Mega Crit's historical nerf/buff philosophy), and cards that have gone untouched for 4+ patches despite community complaints.

What Probably WON'T Change

  • Aeonglass — Just reworked in v0.107.0. Too soon for another change.
  • Co-op Mechanics — No tournament since Devour the Tower. Co-op data pool is thin.
  • Juggernaut — Just buffed (5→6). Mega Crit rarely touches the same card in consecutive patches.
  • Monarch's Gaze — Same reason. Cost reduction (3→2) happened in v0.107.0.
⚠️ T-Minus 48 Hours: This is the final pre-patch forecast. Mega Crit has surprised us before — v0.107.0 was far smaller than anyone predicted. If v0.108.0 is another stability patch, expect the real balance changes in v0.109.0 (~July 2). The bi-weekly cadence may shift to accommodate summer development schedules. Check back June 18 for our day-one v0.108.0 patch analysis and updated tier lists.
