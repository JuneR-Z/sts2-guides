👑 Regent Master Guide: Stars, Forge & Sly Resource Management

📅 Updated Jun 3, 2026 · ⏱️ 16 min read · 🏷️ Master Guide v0.106.1

📊 Community WR: 30.4% — Regent is the most mechanically complex character in STS2. Managing 3 resource systems (Stars, Forge, Sly) simultaneously creates a skill ceiling that rewards mastery but punishes beginners. Top players hit 33%+ A10 WR by learning resource priority.

Regent's Triple Resource System — The Core Challenge

Regent is the only character with three parallel resource systems:

ResourceHow You Generate ItWhat It PowersPriority
⭐ StarsStar-generating cards (Stargaze, Celestial Mark)Starfall finisher, Celestial cardsLate-game win condition
⚒️ ForgeForge cards (Hammer Swing, Armory)Upgrade cards, temporary buffsMid-game tempo
🗡️ SlySly-generating cards (Royal Decree, Intrigue)Sly payoffs, card draw, debuffsEarly-mid game control

The key insight: you cannot scale all three simultaneously. Successful Regent runs pick a primary resource based on early card rewards and commit.

3 Best Regent Builds

1. Starfall Combo — S-Tier (Highest Ceiling)

Generate Stars over multiple turns, then unleash devastating Starfall for 100+ damage. Best boss-killing build, but weak early.

Stargaze (S) · Celestial Mark (S) · Starfall (S) · Constellation (A) · Astral Projection (A) · Night Sky (B)

Game plan: Act 1: survive with Sly cards. Act 2: begin Star generation. Act 3+: Starfall deletes everything. Aim for 20+ Stars per Starfall by Act 3 boss.

2. Sly Control — A-Tier (Most Consistent)

Generate Sly every turn for card draw, energy manipulation, and enemy debuffs. Wins hallway fights effortlessly, but Star damage against bosses is lower.

Royal Decree (S) · Intrigue (A) · Court Schemer (A) · Whispered Secrets (A) · Sly Gambit (B)

Best paired with Silent in co-op for double discard synergy.

3. Royal Guard — B-Tier (Defensive)

Use Forge to upgrade block cards and create a nigh-impenetrable defense. Slowest build but nearly unkillable if set up. Weak to scaling enemies.

Armory (S) · Fortify (A) · Hammer Swing (A) · Royal Guard (A) · Shield Wall (B)

🎯 Key Takeaway: Regent wins by committing to ONE resource path by mid-Act 2. The trap is spreading thin across Stars, Sly, and Forge. Pick your path from the first boss reward, and draft accordingly. Regent is a thinking player's character — reward depth over breadth.
