Regent Sly & Star Build — Command the Battlefield

Build Type: Sly/Star Scaling · Difficulty: Intermediate · Updated May 2026

The Regent is the most tactically complex character in Slay the Spire 2. Their core mechanics — Sly, Stars, and Commands — create a layered decision-making system that rewards careful planning. This guide teaches you how to generate Sly, convert it into Stars, and unleash devastating Starfall finishers.

Understanding Regent's Mechanics

Sly (🧠 Strategy Resource)

Sly is a stacking buff that improves your cards. Many Regent cards have conditional bonuses: "If you have Sly, do X." Each Sly stack represents tactical advantage. You can have unlimited Sly stacks, making it the Regent's primary scaling vector.

Stars (⭐ Burst Resource)

Stars are a currency you accumulate through Sly-generating effects. Unlike Sly, Stars are meant to be spent — primarily via Starfall, which converts all Stars into massive AOE damage. Think of Stars as your "ult meter."

Commands (👑 Tactical Choices)

Each turn, the Regent receives random Commands (bonuses for specific actions). Following a Command generates Sly. Tactical Genius expands your Command options, giving you more flexibility.

Feint → Cunning Plan → Loyal Subject → Starfall = Infinite scaling loop. Every Sly becomes a Star, and Stars get cashed out for screen-clearing damage.

Build Variants

Pure Sly Engine (Balanced)

Priority: Tactical Genius → Feint → Precision Strike → Cunning Plan

The most consistent approach. Stack Sly to ~15-20 and let Precision Strike carry your damage. Master of Arms converts Sly generation into Block, keeping you safe.

Star Combo (Burst)

Priority: Loyal Subject → Cunning Plan → Starfall → Star Ascension

Go all-in on Stars. With Loyal Subject + Star Ascension, you can Starfall every 3-4 turns without losing progress. Best for boss fights.

Command Focus (Utility)

Priority: Tactical Genius+ → Outmaneuver → Cunning Plan → Royal Guard

Generate so much Sly from Commands that your Royal Guard becomes a 30+ damage nuke every time. Outmaneuver gives massive Energy turns.

Stage-by-Stage Game Plan

  1. Act 1 — Take Feint and Precision Strike early. Royal Guard is decent block. First Cunning Plan is an auto-pick.
  2. Act 1 Boss — You should have 3-5 Sly by boss. Precision Strike will be dealing 16+ damage. Don't force Stars yet.
  3. Act 2 — Tactical Genius is your key unlock. With it, every turn generates Sly. Consider Loyal Subject if you're building toward Starfall.
  4. Act 2 Boss — With 10+ Sly and Tactical Genius, your turns generate consistent value. Throne Room on Cunning Plan is a huge power spike.
  5. Act 3 — Time to add Starfall and Star Ascension. You should have 20+ Stars per fight. Starfall easily hits 150+ AOE.
  6. Act 4 — Star Ascension is essential for the Act 4 boss gauntlet. You need to Starfall multiple times. Diplomatic Immunity blocks debuffs.

Relic Synergies

💎 Best Relics for This Build

  • Crown of Stars — Start each combat with 3 Stars. Instant acceleration.
  • Tactical Map — Gain 1 extra Command choice each turn. Sly generation doubled.
  • Royal Scepter — When you gain Sly, gain 1 Block. Passive defense while scaling.
  • Ambition Stone — Stars deal 50% more damage. Direct Starfall buff.
  • Commander's Baton — Following a Command gives +1 Energy. Massive tempo.
💡 Pro Tip: The Feint + Loyal Subject + Star Ascension combo creates a self-sustaining loop. Every Feint gives 1 Sly + 1 Star (via Loyal Subject). After Starfall, you keep all Stars (via Star Ascension). Feint again. Repeat. You never run out of resources.

Common Mistakes

  • Starfalling too early — Don't fire Starfall at 5 Stars. Wait until 15+ for meaningful impact.
  • Ignoring Commands — Commands aren't optional flavor. Following them is how you scale. Track your options every turn.
  • Over-prioritizing Stars without Sly — Stars come from Sly. No Sly = no Stars. Build the engine first.
  • Skipping block cards — Regent's block package (Royal Guard, Courtly Defense, Master of Arms) is strong. Don't go all-in on Sly and forget defense.
