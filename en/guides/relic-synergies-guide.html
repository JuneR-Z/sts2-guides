💎 Best Relic Combos & Synergies: Beyond Individual Rankings

📅 Updated Jun 3, 2026 · ⏱️ 14 min read · 🏷️ Synergy Guide v0.106.1

Individual relic tier lists miss the real power of STS2 — relic combinations. Some relics are B-tier alone but S-tier together. Dead Branch + Corruption isn't two relics; it's a new win condition. This guide maps the 20 most impactful relic synergies in the current meta.

Top 10 Game-Breaking Relic Combos

1. Dead Branch + Corruption (Ironclad) — S++

How it works: Corruption makes skills cost 0 and Exhaust. Dead Branch generates a random card whenever a card exhausts. Every skill you play = 0 energy + random card. Infinite value engine.
Win rate impact: Finding both jumps Ironclad A10 WR from ~31% to ~65%. It's that strong.
How to draft: Take Corruption first. Dead Branch second. Don't take Dead Branch without exhaust — it's C-tier alone.

2. Mummified Hand + Creative AI (Defect) — S++

How it works: Creative AI generates a random Power each turn. Mummified Hand makes the next Power cost 0 after playing one. Every Power you play → next Power is free → infinite Power scaling.
Win rate impact: Solves Defect's scaling problem. Frost trivializes, Lightning kills everything.

3. Tingsha + Tough Bandages (Silent) — S+

How it works: Tingsha deals 3 damage when you discard. Tough Bandages gives 3 Block when you discard. In Sly Discard builds, every discard = 3 dmg + 3 block.
Synergy rating: Transforms Calculated Gamble from "draw 7" to "deal 21 damage + gain 21 block."

4. Kunai + Shuriken (Silent) — S+

How it works: Kunai: +1 Dexterity per 3 attacks. Shuriken: +1 Strength per 3 attacks. Shiv builds trigger both simultaneously.
Synergy rating: 3 shivs = +1 Dex +1 Str. By turn 3, you're a tank with 50+ damage turns.

5. Incense Burner + Fossilized Helix — S

How it works: Incense Burner gives Intangible every 6 turns. Fossilized Helix gives Buffer on turn 1. Together, you have 2 turns of invincibility and can align Burner procs with boss attack patterns.
Best on: Any character. Universal defensive core.

6. Du-Vu Doll + Darkstone Periapt (Ironclad) — S

How it works: Du-Vu Doll: +1 Strength per Curse in deck. Darkstone Periapt: +6 Max HP per Curse. Curses become buffs. Take every curse event.
Build-around: Works with Calling Bell boss relic for instant +3 Strength + 18 Max HP.

7. Snecko Eye + Corruption (Ironclad) — S

Risk: Snecko randomizes costs. But with Corruption (skills cost 0 regardless), you ignore cost randomization for the entire skill pool. High-roll the attack costs, get skills for free.
Caveat: Snecko is a double-edged relic. Only combine with Corruption or significant card draw.

8. Bottled Tornado + Echo Form (Defect) — S

How it works: Bottled Tornado puts a Power in your opening hand. Echo Form is bottled = guaranteed turn-2 double-everything.

9. Paper Krane + Malaise (Silent) — S

How it works: Paper Krane: Weak reduces damage by 50% (instead of 25%). Malaise applies X Weak. X=4 → enemy deals 75% less damage for 4 turns.

10. Strange Spoon + Apparition — S

How it works: Strange Spoon: 50% chance to not exhaust. Apparition: 3(5) copies of Intangible. With spoon, you can have 6+ Intangible turns instead of 3.
Risk: If you don't find Apparition event, Strange Spoon is D-tier.

Trap Synergies — Looks Good, Actually Bad

ComboWhy It's a Trap

Velvet Choker + Shiv buildChoker caps at 6 cards/turn. Shiv build plays 10+. Instantly kills the archetype.
Sozu + AlchemizeSozu disables potion use. Alchemize generates potions. Anti-synergy.
Ectoplasm + Old CoinOld Coin gives 300g. Ectoplasm prevents gold gain for future floors. Wastes the relic slot.
🎯 Key Takeaway: Relic tier lists rate relics in isolation, but the game is played with combinations. A B-tier relic that completes a synergy jumps to S-tier. Draft relics for your existing build, not for a theoretical perfect deck. The best relic combos (Dead Branch + Corruption, Mummified Hand + Creative AI) define entire runs.
