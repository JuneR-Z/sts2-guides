STS2 S-Tier Cards Guide: All 10 Ancient-Rarity Cards Analyzed

~16 min read · Deep analysis of every Ancient card — when to draft, how to build around, and synergy packages.

What Are Ancient (S-Tier) Cards?

Ancient-rarity cards are the rarest and most powerful cards in STS2. Each character has exactly 2 Ancient cards, and they are designed to be run-defining — picking one should fundamentally change how you draft for the rest of the run. Unlike other rarities, Ancient cards often appear from specific events, bosses, or high-rarity reward pools.

IRONCLAD — Corruption & Break

🔴 Corruption Ancient

Cost: 3 Energy | Type: Power | Effect: Skills cost 0 [E]. Whenever you play a Skill, Exhaust it.

Why S-Tier: Corruption is the single most powerful card in all of STS2. It converts your entire Skill suite into 0-cost plays while simultaneously thinning your deck for subsequent shuffles. After playing Corruption, every Skill becomes free block/utility, and your deck permanently shrinks to just Attacks and Powers for the rest of the fight.

Drafting Philosophy: Once you have Corruption, draft every Skill you see. Shrug It Off becomes "0 cost: gain 8 Block, draw 1." Impervious becomes "0 cost: gain 30 Block." The downside (exhaust) is actually upside — your deck gets leaner each cycle.

Key Synergies: Feel No Pain Dark Embrace Body Slam Dead Branch Barricade

Pro Tip: The Corruption + Dead Branch combo is game-breaking. Every exhausted Skill creates a random card, generating infinite value. If you see Dead Branch as Ironclad with Corruption, you've basically won.

⚡ Break Ancient

Cost: 1 Energy | Type: Attack | Effect: Deal 20 damage. Apply 5 Vulnerable.

Why S-Tier: 20 damage + 5 Vulnerable for 1 energy is the most efficient damage card in the game. Break is a one-card engine: it deals massive immediate damage AND sets up all subsequent attacks to deal 50% more. In boss fights, applying 5 Vulnerable means every follow-up attack hits like a truck.

Drafting Philosophy: Break fits into literally every Ironclad deck. It doesn't require build-around — it IS the build-around. Pick it 100% of the time.

Key Synergies: One-Two Punch Tear Asunder Bludgeon Demon Form

SILENT — Wraith Form & Suppress

👻 Wraith Form Ancient

Cost: 3 Energy | Type: Power | Effect: Gain 2 Intangible. At the start of your turn, lose 1 Dexterity.

Why S-Tier: 2 turns of Intangible (reduce all damage to 1) makes Wraith Form the ultimate panic button. In boss fights, it buys you 2 free turns to set up Poison, play your scaling Powers, or burst with Shivs. The Dexterity loss is irrelevant if the fight ends before it matters.

Drafting Philosophy: Wraith Form demands you end fights fast. Pair it with high-damage burst (Catalyst, Grand Finale, Shiv spam). Avoid long, grindy block-plans — the Dexterity drain punishes prolonged fights.

Key Synergies: Catalyst Nightmare Afterimage Well-Laid Plans

⚠️ Warning: Wraith Form at 3 cost is expensive. Always pair with Well-Laid Plans or energy generation (Adrenaline, Tactician). A Wraith Form stuck in your hand on the wrong turn is a dead draw.

🎯 Suppress Ancient

Cost: 0 Energy | Type: Attack | Effect: Deal 11 damage. Apply 3 Weak.

Why S-Tier: 0 cost, 11 damage, AND 3 Weak is absurd value. Suppress provides efficient damage while simultaneously reducing enemy attack damage by 25%. It's free to play, so it never bricks your hand, and it works in any Silent archetype.

Drafting Philosophy: Like Break, Suppress is always-pick. It fits Poison, Shiv, and Discard decks equally well. The Weak application makes hallway fights significantly safer.

Key Synergies: Burst Malaise Leg Sweep

📖 Full Strategy Guide: Suppress →

DEFECT — Biased Cognition & Quadcast

🧠 Biased Cognition Ancient

Cost: 1 Energy | Type: Power | Effect: Gain 4 Focus. At the start of your turn, lose 1 Focus.

Why S-Tier: 4 Focus for 1 energy is an insane rate — it doubles the effectiveness of all your orbs. In a 5-turn fight, you get 4+3+2+1+0 = 10 total Focus-turns. In a 3-turn burst scenario, it's game-winning. The Focus decay creates urgency, but most fights end before you hit zero.

Drafting Philosophy: Biased Cognition rewards aggressive orb-based builds. Lightning spam (Tempest, Storm) converts Focus into damage immediately. Frost orbs (Glacier, Coolheaded) provide massive block before decay. Avoid in long, grindy decks without orb generation.

Key Synergies: Defragment Capacitor Barrage Multi-Cast Echo Form

Pro Tip: Echo Form + Biased Cognition = 8 Focus on turn 1. That's +8 damage per Lightning orb and +16 block per Frost orb. Most hallway fights end in 2 turns with this setup.

📖 Full Strategy Guide: Biased Cognition →

⚡ Quadcast Ancient

Cost: 1 Energy | Type: Skill | Effect: Evoke your rightmost Orb 4 times.

Why S-Tier: For 1 energy, Quadcast triggers your rightmost orb's evoke 4 times. With a Dark orb (accumulated damage), this is 4× stored damage. With Plasma, 8 energy. With Frost, massive block. The versatility makes it the best evoke card in the game.

Key Synergies: Darkness Multi-Cast Fusion Consuming Shadow

NECROBINDER — Forbidden Grimoire & Protector

📖 Forbidden Grimoire Ancient

Cost: 2 Energy | Type: Power | Effect: At the end of combat, you may remove a card from your Deck.

Why S-Tier: FREE CARD REMOVAL AFTER EVERY FIGHT. This is the most powerful scaling effect in the game. Over a 20-fight run, Forbidden Grimoire removes 20+ cards — you can completely eliminate Strikes, Defends, and any temporary cards. By the final boss, your deck is pure gas.

Drafting Philosophy: Play Forbidden Grimoire as early as possible in a fight, then draft aggressively — you can always remove bad picks later. Remove Strikes first, then Defends, then any filler cards. A lean Necrobinder deck with Forbidden Grimoire is nearly unstoppable.

Key Synergies: Death March Seance Reaper Form

💡 Strategy: Forbidden Grimoire + Ascension A5 (Ascender's Bane) is the perfect counter. Remove the curse on fight #1, and you're back to a clean deck immediately.

📖 Full Strategy Guide: Forbidden Grimoire →

🛡️ Protector Ancient

Cost: 1 Energy | Type: Attack | Effect: Osty deals 10 damage. Deals additional damage equal to Osty's Max HP.

Why S-Tier: Osty starts with moderate HP, but after buffs from Calcify, Spur, and other summon cards, Protector can deal 30-50+ damage for 1 energy. In a summon-focused build, this is your primary boss-killer.

Key Synergies: Calcify Spur Necro Mastery Bodyguard

📖 Full Strategy Guide: Protector →

REGENT — The Sealed Throne & Meteor Shower

👑 The Sealed Throne Ancient

Cost: 1 Energy | Type: Power | Effect: Whenever you play a card, gain [S] (1 Star).

Why S-Tier: The Sealed Throne is Regent's engine card — it converts every card play into Star generation. With a typical 5-card turn, you generate 5 free Stars, which can fuel Forge effects, Sovereign Blade scaling, and Genesis synergy. It's the foundation of every high-tier Regent deck.

Drafting Philosophy: Once Sealed Throne is in play, draft cards that spend Stars efficiently: Sovereign Blade, Forge cards, Black Hole (damage per Star spent). Card draw becomes premium since more cards = more Stars.

Key Synergies: Genesis Sovereign Blade Black Hole Furnace Sword Sage

📖 Full Strategy Guide: The Sealed Throne →

☄️ Meteor Shower Ancient

Cost: 0 Energy | Type: Attack | Effect: Deal 14 damage to ALL enemies. Apply 2 Weak and 2 Vulnerable to ALL enemies.

Why S-Tier: 0 cost, AoE damage, AND double debuff — Meteor Shower is Regent's best utility card. It clears minions, sets up Weak for survival, and Vulnerable for follow-up damage, all for zero energy. In multi-enemy fights, no card does more work per mana.

Key Synergies: The Sealed Throne Monarch's Gaze Dying Star
