Silent Sly Discard Build: The #1 Meta Monster 35.6% WR

🏆 Build Guide · Updated Jun 1, 2026 · 12 min read · S+ Tier · 240M+ Global Runs

Table of Contents

Silent's Sly Discard archetype is the highest win rate build in Slay the Spire 2's v0.106.1 meta. With a 35.6% community win rate across 22,560+ tracked runs — and confirmed by Mega Crit's official Neowsletter showing Silent as the top-performing character — this build turns every discard into free cards, every free card into block, and every block into more discards. It's a self-sustaining perpetual-motion engine that plays 15-25 cards per turn while generating enough block to be functionally immortal.

🔥 The Core Loop (Simplified)

Sly (new STS2 keyword) triggers when you play a card that was added to your hand this turn. Paired with the discard engine, cards are constantly entering and leaving your hand. Every Sly Strike played after a discard redraw deals bonus damage. Every Sly Dodge grants block equal to the number of cards you've discarded this turn. The engine feeds itself.

1. Why Sly Discard Dominates (35.6% WR)

The numbers don't lie. Silent leads all characters at 35.6% overall win rate, and Sly Discard is the build driving those numbers. Here's why:

  • Energy-Positive: Tactician+ gives +2 energy per discard. With 3-5 discards per turn, you're netting 6-10 bonus energy. You effectively have 9-13 energy per turn.
  • Card-Positive: Reflex+ draws 2-3 when discarded. Calculated Gamble redraws your whole hand. You see 15-25 cards per turn.
  • Block-Positive: Sly Dodge and After Image generate block from card plays. 15+ cards = 15+ free block plus Sly bonuses.
  • Damage-Scalable: Sly Strike deals 8→12→16 damage as you chain discards. Finisher caps the turn at 70-100 damage.
  • Consistent: The discard engine thins your deck in real-time. By turn 3, you're cycling your entire deck every turn.
📊 By the Numbers: A fully assembled Sly Discard deck averages 140-180 damage/turn and 25-40 block/turn from turn 3 onward. That's enough to outscale every boss in the game, including the Heart on A20.

2. The Dual-Engine Breakdown

Sly Discard runs on two interlocking engines. Understanding how they feed each other is the key to piloting this build.

Engine A — The Discard Core (Energy & Draw)

🔄 Discard Loop Components

Step 1: Play Acrobatics → Draw 3-4, discard 1 Tactician+ → Gain 2 energy.
Step 2: Play Calculated Gamble+ → Discard hand (triggering 2x Tactician = 4 energy, 1x Reflex = draw 3). Redraw full hand.
Step 3: New hand contains fresh Sly cards. Play them. Cycle back to Step 1.

Engine B — The Sly Package (Damage & Block)

Sly is a new STS2 keyword: "Sly X: Y" means "If this card was added to your hand this turn, do Y instead of X." Since the discard engine constantly adds cards to your hand (draw, Calculated Gamble redraw, Reflex procs), every card you play potentially triggers its Sly effect.

🃏 Key Sly Interactions

Sly Strike — Deal 6 damage. Sly: Deal 12 damage. If you've discarded 3+ cards this turn, deal 18 instead.
Sly Dodge — Gain 5 Block. Sly: Gain 8 Block plus 2 additional Block for each card discarded this turn.
Sly Gambit — Draw 2 cards. Sly: Draw 3 cards and reduce their cost by 1 this turn.

3. The Sly Package — New STS2 Cards

These are the new cards that separate Sly Discard from the traditional Shiv/Discard hybrid. They're unique to STS2 and form the backbone of this archetype.

Must-Have Sly Cards (S-Tier)

  • Sly Strike — 1-cost Attack. Deal 6 damage. Sly: Deal 12 (+3 per discard this turn). With 5+ discards, this routinely hits for 25-30 damage for 1 energy. Take 2-3 copies.
  • Sly Dodge — 1-cost Skill. Gain 5 Block. Sly: Gain 8 + (2 × discards this turn). After a Calculated Gamble turn, this gives 18-22 Block for 1 energy. Your primary defensive card.
  • Sly Gambit — 0-cost Skill. Draw 2. Sly: Draw 3, reduce cost by 1 this turn. This is the engine's lubricant — it finds your pieces while making them cheaper. Upgrade to Innate.
  • Shadow Step — 0-cost Skill. Discard 1, gain 4 Block. Sly: Discard 2, gain 8 Block, draw 1. Cheap discard trigger + block + draw. Excellent at all stages.

Traditional Discard Engine (A-Tier)

  • Tactician+ — +2 energy when discarded. Run 2 copies. The energy backbone.
  • Reflex+ — Draw 2-3 when discarded. Run 1-2 copies.
  • Calculated Gamble+ — 0-cost, discard hand, redraw. The combo enabler. Run 1-2 copies.
  • Acrobatics — Draw 3-4, discard 1. Primary draw and discard trigger.
  • Prepared+ — Draw 2, discard 2. Efficient double-trigger.
⚠️ Don't Overload on Discard: A common mistake is taking every discard card offered. You only need 6-8 discard engine cards total. The rest should be Sly payoffs and defensive tools. Too many discard enablers without Sly finishers leaves you cycling cards for no reason.

5. Best Relics & Sly Synergies

  • Wrist Blade (S+) — 0-cost Attacks deal +4 damage. Sly Gambit and Shadow Step become 0-cost. Combined with Sly Strike (often reduced to 0-1 cost), this doubles your damage floor.
  • Tingsha (S) — Deal 3 damage per discard. With 8-12 discards per turn, that's 24-36 passive damage. This alone wins hallway fights.
  • Tough Bandages (S) — Gain 3 Block per discard. 8-12 discards = 24-36 passive Block. Stacked with Sly Dodge + After Image, you're generating 40-60 block per turn.
  • Band of Sly (S) — New STS2 relic: Sly effects trigger twice. This doubles Sly Strike damage, Sly Dodge block, and Sly Gambit draw. The single most impactful relic for this build.
  • Hovering Kite (A) — Gain 1 energy per 2 discards each turn. Free energy from your engine.
  • Pocketwatch (A) — If you play 3 or fewer cards, draw 3 next turn. Synergizes poorly — you're playing 15+ cards. Skip this.

6. Turn-by-Turn Gameplay

Here's how a typical fight plays out with the engine assembled:

  1. Turn 1 — Setup:

    Play Well-Laid Plans to hold Calculated Gamble. Play Sly Gambit (Innate) for draw + cost reduction. Play any 0-cost cards in hand. Play Tools of the Trade if available. End turn with Tactician and Reflex in hand if possible.

  2. Turn 2 — Engine Start:

    Play Acrobatics, discarding Tactician/Reflex. Play Sly Strike (now Sly-triggered, hitting for 12-18). Play Shadow Step for more discards. You've now discarded 4-5 times. Sly effects are online.

  3. Turn 3 — Full Throttle:

    Play Calculated Gamble+. Your entire hand goes to discard (triggering ALL Tactician/Reflex effects). Redraw a fresh 7-10 cards. Play every Sly card — they're all newly added to hand, so every Sly effect triggers. Sly Dodge for 16-22 Block. Sly Strike x3 for 45-60 damage. Finisher for 70+. This turn alone does 120-150 damage while generating 30-40 block.

  4. Turn 4+ — Infinite Loop:

    Your discard pile is now your entire deck. Play another Acrobatics or Prepared to restart the cycle. You'll see every card every turn. Bosses die in 2-3 cycles.

7. Ascension Climbing Strategy

Sly Discard scales remarkably well through Ascension levels because it doesn't rely on any single card and generates its own resources:

  • A0-A9: Build is borderline unfair. You can force Sly Discard almost every run and win. Prioritize Sly Gambit over everything.
  • A10-A16: Enemies hit harder. You need Sly Dodge by mid-Act 2 or you'll bleed out. After Image becomes mandatory against multi-hit enemies.
  • A17-A19: Enemies have more HP and better AI. You need Terror or Finisher to close fights before enemy scaling outpaces you. Two Tacticians are non-negotiable.
  • A20: Double boss gauntlet. The deck that beats Act 3 boss #1 must also beat boss #2. Save a potion. Band of Sly relic is the difference between a close win and a crushing victory.

8. Boss & Elite Matchups

  • Gremlin Nob: Hard. Skills trigger his Strength buff. Play only Sly Strike and attacks. Save Sly Gambit for after the fight. Bring a potion.
  • Lagavulin: Easy. You have time to set up. Play all your Powers (Tools of the Trade, Well-Laid Plans) before waking it. Then unleash the full engine.
  • Book of Stabbing: Easy with After Image. Every multi-hit is negated by your card-play block. Sly Dodge shines here.
  • Collector: Medium. Kill minions with Sly Strike chains. Don't waste Finisher on minions.
  • Time Eater: Tricky but winnable. Play exactly 12 cards/turn. The key: play 11 cards, then end with a massive Finisher. Don't waste card plays on low-value cycling.
  • Awakened One: Hard. Powers trigger its Strength. Play only essential Powers (1 After Image, 1 Tools). Win with raw damage.
  • The Heart: After Image + Sly Dodge are mandatory. Beat of Death deals 2 damage per card played. You need to generate 30+ block per turn passively. With Band of Sly, this fight becomes winnable. Without it, it's a 50/50.

The Sly Discard build is the evolution of Silent's discard archetype. Where traditional Shiv/Discard relies on sustained chip damage from Accuracy-buffed Shivs, Sly Discard delivers tremendous burst damage through Sly-triggered attacks while generating comparable block. The result is faster kills, less chip damage taken, and a higher win rate at all Ascension levels.
