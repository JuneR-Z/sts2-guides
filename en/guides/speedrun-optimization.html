Speedrun Optimization Guide: How to Clear A0 in Under 10 Minutes

⏱️ Speedrun Guide · Updated Jun 13, 2026 · 6 min read · Post-Speedrun Marathon Meta (Jun 7)

The STS2 Speedrun Marathon (June 7) proved that sub-8-minute A0 clears are possible. Ironclad's Exhaust Engine dominates the speedrun category, with Silent and Necrobinder competing for the #2 spot. This guide distills the marathon's top strategies into actionable tips for your own speedrun attempts — whether you're aiming for a personal best or competing on the Speedrun.com leaderboard.

Ironclad Speedrun Build: Exhaust Engine Turbo

The marathon-winning build is deceptively simple:

  • Core: Exhaust Engine (deal 5 per exhaust, buffed to 6 in v0.107.0) + any exhaust cards. Every card you exhaust deals damage — you win by exhausting your deck.
  • Accelerators: Offering (draw 3, gain 2 energy, lose HP — who cares, you're speedrunning), Battle Trance (draw 3), Seeing Red (gain 2 energy).
  • Draft Priority: Exhaust Engine > Offering > Battle Trance > any card with "Exhaust" in the text. Skip block cards entirely — you out-damage enemies before they hit you.

⚡ Speedrunner's Combo

Turn 1: Offering (draw 3, +2 energy) → Exhaust Engine (on field) → True Grit (exhaust a Strike, deal 6 dmg) → Battle Trance (draw 3 more) → Havoc (play top card free, exhaust it, deal 6 more dmg). 5 cards played, 3 exhausted, 18 damage dealt, 7 cards drawn — in 1 turn. Repeat this loop for 3-4 turns and the boss is dead.

Speedrun Settings & Optimization

  1. Fast Mode: Enable in Settings. Doubles animation speed. Non-negotiable — saves ~90 seconds per run.
  2. Disable Screen Shake: Eliminates visual noise. Saves ~30 seconds of cumulative distraction over a run.
  3. Keyboard-Only Controls: Mouse clicks are slow. Memorize hotkeys: 1-9 for cards, E to end turn, Space to confirm. Shaves ~60 seconds per run.
  4. Skip Events: Events have unskippable text animations. Path through ? rooms only if they're unavoidable. Combat rooms are faster (enemy intents are skippable in Fast Mode).
  5. Campfire = Always Upgrade: Resting wastes time (animation). If you're speedrunning correctly, you shouldn't need to heal anyway.

Pathing: The Fastest Route

Speedrun pathing prioritizes minimum combats, maximum campfires:

  • Act 1: Path through 3-4 combats, 2 campfires. Avoid Elites — they take too long even if you win. Aim to reach the boss in under 3 minutes.
  • Act 2: Path through 4-5 combats, 2 campfires. The City has the fewest event nodes — lean into it. Collect your Rare card reward.
  • Act 3: Path through 5-6 combats, 2 campfires. No Elites. The boss fight should be under 2 minutes with a fully upgraded Exhaust Engine.

Community Speedrun Categories

  • A0 Any%: Fastest clear at Ascension 0 with any character. The most competitive category. Ironclad ~7:42 WR.
  • A20 Any%: Fastest clear at Ascension 20. Necrobinder dominates (~25 min WR) because Doom Execute ignores HP scaling.
  • Character-Specific: Separate leaderboards for each character. Perfect for competing on your main.
  • No Neow's Blessing: No starting bonus. Adds ~30-60 seconds but tests raw skill.
💡 Speedrunner Pro Tip — Reset Criteria: If you don't have Exhaust Engine by Floor 8 (end of Act 1), reset the run. Without it, Ironclad's clear time drops to ~15+ minutes. The marathon winner reset ~7 times before their WR run — speedrunning is as much about efficient resets as fast play.
