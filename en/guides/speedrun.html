Speedrun Guide: Fastest Classes, Routing, Settings & Benchmarks

Everything you need to know about beating STS2 as fast as possible — character tier list, routing, time-saving techniques, key bindings, and target benchmarks.

Advanced Speedrun Tech 🕐 11 min read 📅 Updated: May 2026

Slay the Spire 2 speedruns are a test of decisiveness, muscle memory, and risk tolerance. Unlike traditional gameplay, speedruns value speed over safety — you'll take fights faster, skip campfire animations, and path aggressively. The world record for STS2 (Any%) sits at approximately 4–6 minutes at the top level.

Optimal Speedrun Builds (Any%)

Ironclad: Whirlwind Rush

  • Core cards: Whirlwind+, Flex+, Offering+, Double Tap+
  • Relics: Chemical X (doubles Whirlwind), Akabeko, Bag of Marbles, Necronomicon
  • Gameplan: Path to campfires → upgrade Whirlwind → path into ? rooms for events → skip Elites → buy Flex at shops → Whirlwind every fight in 1 turn
  • Boss strategy: Whirlwind on turn 1. If it doesn't die, Offering → Whirlwind again. Bosses die in 2–3 turns.
🏆 WR Route (Ironclad): Act 1: take 3 combats, upgrade Whirlwind at first fire, grab Flex/Offering if offered. Act 2: hit ? rooms, shops for ChemX/Necro. Act 3: same. Kill everything with Whirlwind. That's the route.

Watcher: Stance Burst

  • Core cards: Tantrum+, Blasphemy+, Ragnarok+, Conclude+, Vault+
  • Relics: Bag of Marbles, Akabeko, Pen Nib (time it!), Du-Vu Doll
  • Gameplan: Enter Wrath → play attacks → enter Calm (to leave Wrath safely or gain energy) → repeat. Blasphemy is a "kill this turn" button.
  • Vault is the best speedrun card in the game: "Skip the enemy turn, take an extra turn." On bosses, Vault→Blasphemy→Ragnarok ends the fight.
⚠️ Watcher Speedrun Warning: Blasphemy's "die next turn" means you must end the fight on that turn. Double-check lethal before pressing Blasphemy. A dead run = 0 speedrun time.

Silent: Shiv Rush

  • Core cards: Blade Dance+, Accuracy+, Thousand Cuts+, After Image+
  • Relics: Shuriken, Kunai, Wrist Blade, Ninja Scroll, Dead Branch
  • Gameplan: Upgrade Blade Dance → take every Shiv generator → Accuracy for +4/6 damage per hit → Thousand Cuts for AoE chip → enemy dies while you generate block from After Image
  • Animation note: Shiv spam has longer animation times than Whirlwind. This is Silent's main speedrun weakness.

Optimal Routing (Any% Ironclad)

Act 1 Route

  1. Floors 1–3: Fight 2–3 hallway combats. Take Whirlwind (highest priority), Anger, Flex. Skip all other cards.
  2. Floor 4–5: Campfire → Upgrade Whirlwind. This is non-negotiable. Whirlwind must be upgraded.
  3. Floors 6–10: Path through ? rooms. Events are faster than fights. Skip Elites — relic reward is not worth the 30–45 second fight.
  4. Floor 11–14: More ? rooms. If forced into combat, Whirlwind kills in 1–2 turns.
  5. Floor 15: Shop (if available). Buy Flex, Offering, Chemical X, Bag of Marbles. Remove a Defend.
  6. Floor 16: Campfire → Upgrade Flex or Offering.
  7. Floor 17: Act Boss.

Act 2 Route

  1. Priority 1: Path through ? rooms and shops. Avoid combats when possible.
  2. Shop buy list: Chemical X > Necronomicon > Akabeko > Du-Vu Doll > Bag of Marbles
  3. Card picks: Double Tap (doubles Whirlwind), second Whirlwind, Offering. Skip everything else.
  4. Campfires: Upgrade Double Tap. If you picked a second Whirlwind, upgrade it too.
  5. Avoid Elites — not worth the time on Any%.

Act 3 Route

  1. ? rooms > Shops > Campfires > Combats. By Act 3 your Whirlwind should be 1-shotting hallways. If not, you need more Strength or ChemX.
  2. Final shop: Buy potions (Flex Potion, Duplication Potion, Cultist Potion). These secure the Boss kill.
  3. Final campfire: Heal if HP is low. You should be at ~25–40 HP at minimum for the Boss.
  4. Act 3 Boss: Flex → Whirlwind → Offering → Whirlwind → dead. If you have ChemX, this is a turn 1 kill.

Common Speedrun Mistakes

  1. Taking too many cards. Your deck should be 10–15 cards maximum. Every extra card dilutes Whirlwind draws.
  2. Reading card text. You should know every card by its art and energy cost. Reading costs 1–2 seconds per reward screen — that's 20–30 seconds per run.
  3. Pathing into Elites. Elites take 30–60 seconds to kill. A relic is not worth 30–60 seconds in Any% speedruns. Skip them.
  4. Not upgrading Whirlwind immediately. Unupgraded Whirlwind does 5 dmg/hit. Upgraded = 8 dmg/hit. That's a 60% damage increase.
  5. Forgetting Fast Mode. Fast Mode is ~40% of your total time savings. If you forget to enable it, you've already lost.
  6. Using mouse for everything. Keyboard-only (number keys + E) is significantly faster than mouse clicking.
  7. Picking Silent/Defect for Any%. They're objectively slower. Play Ironclad or Watcher if you care about time.
  8. Forgetting End Turn shortcut. Pressing "E" to end your turn saves ~0.5 seconds per turn, which adds up to ~90 seconds over a full run.

A20 Speedrun Differences

A20 speedrunning requires a fundamentally different approach from Any%:

  • You can't skip Elites. You need relics to survive A20. Path into 1–2 Elites per Act.
  • You need a real block plan. Whirlwind-ony won't cut it. You need Shrug It Off, Flame Barrier, or Reaper for sustain.
  • Card picks are harder. On A20, hallway fights are harder and you need a balanced deck. You can't just pick Whirlwind and skip everything else.
  • Time target: Sub-25 minutes is excellent. Sub-20 is world-class for A20 speedruns.
⏱️ A20 Speedrun vs. Casual A20: A casual A20 run takes 45–90 minutes. An A20 speedrun with Fast Mode, key bindings, and routing knowledge takes 15–25 minutes — same difficulty, 3× faster.

  • Watcher God Seed: Early Tantrum or Ragnarok. Blasphemy by Act 2.
  • Practice seed mindset: Run the same seed 10× and optimize your every decision. Time each section. Find your milliseconds.

Key Takeaways

  1. Enable Fast Mode. This alone cuts your run time by ~40%. Do it now.
  2. Learn keyboard shortcuts. Number keys to select cards, E to end turn. Never click.
  3. Play Ironclad or Watcher. They are objectively the fastest characters for Any% speedruns.
  4. Whirlwind is your win condition. Upgrade it immediately. Buy ChemX at every opportunity.
  5. Skip card rewards. Your deck should be lean (10–15 cards). Only take cards that directly support your win condition.
  6. Practice one seed to perfection. Speedrunning is about optimization, not variety. Nail one seed before trying random seeds.
