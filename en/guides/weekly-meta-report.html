STS2 Weekly Meta Report: v0.106.1 (May 26 — June 1, 2026)

📊 Meta Report · Updated May 31, 2026 · 6 min read · Week 22 of EA

Welcome to the STS2Guides Weekly Meta Report. This week marks two major milestones: 2.4 billion total runs completed across all platforms (official Mega Crit data from Neowsletter #23), and the May 29 Main Branch Bug Fix Rollup — the largest stability patch since Major Update 1 with 44+ fixes. Necrobinder Doom remains the #1 A20 strategy, the Devour the Tower tournament has crowned new meta innovations, and Reddit consensus is calling for Aeonglass Crystal Armor tuning.

Biggest Meta Shifts This Week

1. Necrobinder Doom Claims #1 Spot

The Doom Execute build has jumped from #3 to #1 in A20 clears. The reason is simple: as players push higher Ascensions, enemy HP inflation makes Doom's percentage-based execute more valuable. At A20, Doom effectively deals 40-50% of a boss's HP in one card — no other build matches that efficiency.

Community sentiment: "Doom is broken at A20" is the top-voted Reddit post this week with 1.2k upvotes. Expect a nerf in v0.107.

2. Ironclad Exhaust Still Strong, But Falling

Exhaust Engine dropped 1.8% in win rate. The Infested Prism rework is partly to blame — the new Parasite Burst mechanic punishes slow-scaling decks that take 8+ turns to set up. Exhaust is still S-Tier, but it's no longer the undisputed king.

3. Defect Frost Wall Rising

Defect's win rate jumped 2.1% as players discovered that Buffer + Glacier completely trivializes Aeonglass's Phase 2 Crystal Armor. The "wait out armor" strategy (see Aeonglass Guide) was popularized by streamer Jorbs this week, and Defect executes it best.

4. Regent Post-Buff: Promising But Inconsistent

Regent's win rate nearly doubled (+3.6%) after the v0.106 buffs to Parry and Arsenal. However, the character still struggles with energy consistency. The subreddit is split: half say Regent is now viable at A20, half say the buffs weren't enough.

This Week's Biggest Story: May 29 Main Branch Rollup

On May 29, Mega Crit pushed a massive bug fix rollup to the main branch — 44+ fixes touching every character, every act, and co-op mode. While not a content patch, this is the largest stability update since Major Update 1 (v0.103.2). Key fixes that affect gameplay:

  • Ironclad: Armaments+ no longer double-offers upgraded cards via Generosity+ (fixed a rare infinite upgrade exploit).
  • Defect: Scrape fixed — no longer discards 0-energy Astral-cost cards.
  • Regent: Summon timing fix — Sovereign Blade now placed in hand before forging, enabling combo lines.
  • Co-op: Parr's Legion, Gauntlet, Driftwood interactions fixed; Steam 5008 timeout properly messaged.
  • Enemies: Hallucinatory Mirror negative Strength carryover, Pickpocket Funnel softlock, Ritual Beast stun/music sync all fixed.

Devour the Tower Tournament Recap (May 25)

The first major STS2 community tournament concluded on May 25, hosted by FrostPrime and Otzdarva with 34 streamers competing. Key takeaways:

  • Winning strategy: Necrobinder Doom Execute took 6 of the top 10 finishes — concrete proof of its A20 dominance.
  • Defect resurgence: Frost Wall + Buffer builds placed 2nd and 4th, exploiting Aeonglass's Phase 2 vulnerability.
  • Regent upset: One top-8 finish with a Parry + Starfall hybrid — showing the character has untapped potential despite the 8.4% average win rate.
  • Silent disappointment: No Silent decks made top 8, confirming that post-Shatter nerf, the character lacks a dominant build.
  • Community stats: Tournament had 120K+ concurrent Twitch viewers across all streams — STS2's biggest streaming event since launch week.

Predictions for v0.107 (Expected June 3-4)

  • Necrobinder Doom nerf: Execute threshold cap reduced from 55% to 45%, or Death Sentence stacks reduced from 3 to 2. Tournament dominance makes this almost certain.
  • Aeonglass adjustment: Crystal Armor duration reduced from 2 turns to 1, or Phase 2 HP reduced from 180 to 150.
  • Regent buff: Additional energy generation card or reduced Starfall cost — the tournament's lone Regent top-8 shows promise if energy is fixed.
  • Silent buff: After zero top-8 tournament finishes, expect Shiv or Discard buffs.
  • New enemy: Community dataminers found references to a "Void Herald" enemy type — possibly Act 4 content.
💡 Pro Tip — Meta Chasing: Don't chase the meta blindly. Necrobinder Doom is strong, but if you've never played Necrobinder, you'll lose more runs learning the character than you'd gain from the "OP" build. Play what you know — Ironclad Exhaust and Silent Poison are still S-Tier and more forgiving for intermediate players.
