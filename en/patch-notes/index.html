Slay the Spire 2 Patch Notes

📋 Update History · Last updated: Jun 16, 2026 · 12 min read
🔮 Next Patch Expected: ~June 18, 2026 — v0.108.0 beta (bi-weekly from v0.107.0 on June 4). Just 2 days away. Main branch merge of v0.107.0 likely before then. v0.107.0 was a stability-first patch; expect the next one to return to more substantial balance changes and possibly Act 4 content. Steam Live: 68.6K concurrent, 85K June average, ~−25% MoM (Steambase, Jun 16). Decline rate stabilizing — playerbase plateau suggests loyal core retained.
Early Access — actively updated. This page tracks every balance change. Tier List and card ratings are updated within 24h of each patch.
📊 Official Stats (May 2026 Neowsletter): 240M+ global runs played · 25% overall win rate · A0 16% vs STS1 9% · A10 ~17% vs STS1 A20 3%. All characters nearly equal in popularity (Ironclad & Silent slightly ahead).
🏆 Community Event: Devour the Tower — the first community-led StS2 tournament featuring 34 streamers in 4-player co-op (May 25, 2026). Read the full meta analysis and co-op team building lessons in our Weekly Meta Report.

Update Timeline

v0.107.0 — Stability & Groundwork Patch
June 4, 2026 · Beta Branch

A deliberately smaller patch laying the groundwork for beta→main merge. 5 card changes, Aeonglass rework, The Boot relic buff, new art & animations, 20+ bug fixes.

  • Buff Ironclad Juggernaut 5(7) → 6(8) dmg.
  • Buff Regent Monarch's Gaze cost 3(2) → 2(1).
  • Nerf Regent Reflect block 16(20) → 15(20).
  • Buff Necrobinder Sic 'Em summons 2(3) → 3(4).
  • Buff Necrobinder The Scythe dmg 3(4) → 4(5).
  • Rework Aeonglass no longer applies Ebb debuff; now gains Block on Ebb action instead.
  • Buff The Boot relic: damage increase now applies to Osty attacks.
  • Art New Defect charge animation, Epoch art (Devastation, The 12, The Giant), Soul Nexus VFX.
  • Fix 20+ bug fixes: Steam Cloud overwrite, Aeonglass Wither card exploit, Spectral Knight Ethereal loss, Pantograph+Red Skull interaction, co-op desync crashes, mod save corruption, performance stutter reduction.
→ Week 24 meta analysis & patch impact
May Bug Fixes — Main Branch Rollup
May 29, 2026 · Main Branch

44+ bug fixes from beta merged to main. Largest stability patch since Major Update 1.

  • Fix General: Crash when applying powers to dying entities, Linux ICU/audio bugs, controller navigation for shops/rest sites/card grid, Pandora's Box confirm hotkey blocked, Festering Wounds killing players, potion removal softlock, duplication bosses on saves with removed bosses, multiple Plunder Map cards not all removed after map quest.
  • Fix Ironclad: Armaments+ no longer re-offers upgraded cards through Generosity+.
  • Fix Defect: Scrape no longer discards 0-energy Astral-cost cards. Devour Shadow tooltip spacing fixed.
  • Fix Regent: Summon now places existing Sovereign Blade into hand before forging, not after.
  • Fix Enemies: Lagavulin Matriarch Zzz particles, Shrieker VFX on reflect death, Zapper tooltip, Living Mist smoke, Ritual Beast stun/music timing, Withering Touch still giving Strength after stun, Pickpocket Funnel softlock, Hallucinatory Mirror negative Strength carryover.
  • Fix Events: Infected Mecha dropping wrong character cards, Boxing Bout cannot discard potions, cannot give Stale Potion to Merchant.
  • Fix Ancients: Crash on choosing Ancient relic option, Leafy Poultice no longer transforms Neow-gifted upgraded Strikes/Defends.
  • Fix Co-op: Parr's Legion & Gauntlet relic triggers, 1-player co-op launch bug, HP bar overflow, player name bracket errors, Driftwood softlock, Steam 5008 timeout messaging.

v0.106.0 — Enemy Balance & Card Updates

Major enemy rework patch with card changes across all characters.

  • Buff Ironclad Entrench now counts Dexterity.
  • Buff Defect Death March damage increased.
  • Nerf Regent Sealed Throne lost Innate keyword.
  • Nerf Silent Shatter damage halved.
  • Nerf Haunted Ship lost Ram Speed attack — significantly easier.
  • Fix Mummified Hand now prioritizes highest-cost cards in hand.

v0.104.0 — Ironclad & Regent Card Reworks
April 23, 2026 · Beta Branch

Ascension difficulty overhaul and card design pivot for two characters.

  • New Conflagration reworked: all-enemy AOE, 2 damage x 4(5) hits.
  • New Drum of Battle: draw 2, gain 2(3) energy when exhausted.
  • New Parry becomes a Power: Sovereign Blade gains Block (scales with Dexterity).
  • Buff Lower Ascension easier, high Ascension harder. New tutorial popup on first clear.

  • Fix Axebots reworked to single stronger enemy; Terminal no longer drops HP below 1.

v0.103.2 — Major Update #1 (Main Branch)
April 17, 2026 · Main Branch

One month of beta content merged to main. Doormaker overhauls, new Neow relics, multi-character card reworks.

  • New 5 Neow starter relics: Hefty Tablet, Neow's Talisman, Neow's Bones, Phial Holster, Winged Boots.
  • New Ironclad card Not Yet (2-cost, heal 10(13), Exhaust).
  • New Ascension 6 rework: Gloom → Inflation (increased card removal costs).

  • New Phobia Mode joins main branch.
